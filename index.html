My Interests

Cats 🐈

I'm a cat person. I love how they're cute, feisty, and unapologetically sassy. They're independent, observant, and somehow manage to act like they own the place — which, honestly, they probably do. What I admire most is that you can't force affection from a cat. You have to earn their trust, and when you do, it feels special. There's something comforting about their quiet presence, soft purring, and random bursts of chaos. These fur babies may act aloof, but they're full of personality and warmth once they choose you.
